Understanding the curve

The curve follows the benchmarks explained in Jeff Sauro’s 5 Ways to Interpret a SUS Score. Its score-to-percentile points come from the SUS percentile lookup in Sauro and Lewis’s companion material; values between points are estimated by linear interpolation.

The letters follow MeasuringU’s curved SUS grading scale, with plus and minus grades grouped under A, B or C.

How scoring works

The standard SUS scoring rule converts ten ratings into a score from 0 to 100.

STEP 01

Odd-numbered statements

Subtract 1 from the selected rating for questions 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9.

STEP 02

Even-numbered statements

Subtract the selected rating from 5 for questions 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10.

STEP 03

Participant and group scores

Add the ten adjusted values, multiply by 2.5, then average the completed participant scores.
